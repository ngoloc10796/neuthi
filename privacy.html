Privacy Policy

Nếu Thì Publisher · Effective 28 October 2026

1. Summary

Nếu Thì Publisher is used only by the owner of the Nếu Thì channel to post their own videos. It collects no data from viewers or other people, has no website accounts, uses no analytics or advertising trackers, and never sells or shares data.

2. Data the app receives from TikTok

  • Basic profile (scope user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected before posting.
  • Posting (scope video.publish): the creator settings TikTok returns (such as allowed privacy levels and maximum video length), used to post the owner's video correctly.
  • Own video statistics (scope video.list): view, like, comment and share counts of the owner's own videos, used only for a private performance report on the owner's computer.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok after the owner signs in.

The app does not read followers, messages, comments, contacts or any other user's data.

3. Where data is stored

Tokens and posting records are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server run by the app. Videos are sent directly to TikTok through the official Content Posting API.

4. Sharing

No data is shared with third parties, except the video and caption sent to TikTok when the owner chooses to post.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects. To delete them, remove the local token file and revoke access in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. Revoking access ends the app's access immediately.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by the adult channel owner.
